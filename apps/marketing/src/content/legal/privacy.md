PolyScript is committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account information (email, name, company)
- Audio files you upload for transcription
- Generated transcripts and metadata
- Usage analytics and performance metrics

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- Process and transcribe your audio
- Provide access to transcripts
- Improve service accuracy and performance
- Send service-related communications

## 3. Data Security

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel
- We maintain regular security audits
- Data is retained according to your plan settings

## 4. Data Sharing

- We do not sell your data to third parties
- Subprocessors may process data on our behalf under strict contracts
- Law enforcement requests are reviewed and complied with as required

## 5. Your Rights

- Access your data and export it
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Opt out of non-essential communications
- Review and update account settings

## 6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and optional analytics cookies to improve the service.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in jurisdictions where we operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 8. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, contact privacy@polyscript.io
